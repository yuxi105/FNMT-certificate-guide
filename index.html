Your first FNMT digital certificate: checklist

This guide is for you if all four of these are true:

  • You are a non-EU citizen with a valid passport and TIE
  • You are in Barcelona
  • You are using a Windows computer or a Mac
  • You are using the Chrome browser

If any of these is not true, this guide has not been checked for your case yet. For example, EU citizens prove their identity with the EU registration certificate (Certificado de Registro de la Unión Europea) together with their NIE, which this guide does not cover. Please follow the official FNMT pages instead: how to get the certificate and which documents prove your identity with an NIE.

Where this guide stops: when your certificate shows as valid (stage 5) and you have a safe backup (stage 6). Using the certificate on tax or other government websites is not covered, apart from a pointer in stage 5.

The certificate and the official FNMT instructions are free. This page only puts the steps in order and links to the official pages.

Never type your NIE, passwords or codes on this site. Enter them only on the official FNMT pages.

How this page learns: if you press "I'm stuck here" in a stage, it asks three multiple-choice questions and shows any checked fix. It then makes a short report with only your choices (no name, no NIE). Nothing is sent unless you copy and send it yourself. Reports help me write fixes for the next person, and a person checks every fix against the official FNMT page before it appears here.

All six stages done. Your certificate works in Chrome and is backed up.
