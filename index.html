Your first FNMT digital certificate: checklist

This guide is for you if all four of these are true:

  • You are a non-EU citizen with a valid passport and TIE
  • You are in Barcelona
  • You are using a Windows computer or a Mac
  • You are using the Chrome browser

The certificate and the official FNMT instructions are free. This page only puts the steps in order and links to the official pages.

Never type your NIE, passwords or codes on this site. Enter them only on the official FNMT pages.

All six stages done. Your certificate works in Chrome and is backed up.
